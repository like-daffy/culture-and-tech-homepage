English-Thai Learn Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-27

English-Thai Learn is provided by Chan Baek (Culture & Tech), referred to as “we,” “us,” or “our.” This policy covers the English-Thai Learn app for iPhone from the App Store, including the Unlimited listening purchase, and explains what happens when you contact us or visit this policy page or the support page.

English-Thai Learn works on your iPhone. Its words, sentences and recordings are included in the app, so searching, reading and listening need no internet connection, and the app sends nothing to us. It has no English-Thai Learn account, advertisements, tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. The app itself makes no internet requests; Apple's StoreKit framework, which is part of iOS, communicates with the App Store for prices, purchases and restores (Section 2).

1. Information stored on your iPhone

The app keeps a few records in its own preferences on your iPhone:

  • Favorites: the identifiers of the words and sentences you have starred, in the order you starred them.
  • Settings: the Thai font, the theme and the speaking style you chose with the buttons in the header.
  • Today's free plays: how many voice plays you have started today and the date they belong to (Section 2).
  • Purchase state: whether Unlimited listening is owned, as last confirmed through Apple (Section 2), so that it also works offline.
  • Rating request: whether the app still owes the one rating prompt that follows a purchase, and when it asked (Section 3).

None of these records identifies you, and none leaves your iPhone through the app. The app does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos, and it asks for no iOS permissions. It does not record you; the recordings you hear are part of the app.

The records stay until you change them in the app or delete the app. Favorites can be removed with the star, settings changed with the header buttons, and the daily count starts again after midnight in your iPhone's time zone. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes its data; offloading the app keeps it. If you back up your iPhone, the records are included in the backup, which you control through iOS. We cannot see, change or delete records stored only on your iPhone.

2. Free plays, purchases and restoring

Each day you can start up to 10 voice plays free of charge; the app counts them on your iPhone (Section 1). Searching, reading and favorites are not limited.

Unlimited listening is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. The app uses Apple's StoreKit framework to show the price in the currency of your App Store country or region, to complete the purchase, and to check whether your Apple Account owns it: when the app opens, when it returns to the front, and when you tap Restore Purchases. These checks run on your iPhone with Apple; the app does not send transactions to a developer-operated verification server, and we operate no server. Once a purchase is confirmed, the app remembers it (Section 1) and checks again with Apple on later launches, so a refund or revocation ends unlimited listening.

Restore Purchases asks Apple to sync the purchases of your Apple Account; iOS may ask you to sign in. We do not receive your Apple Account, its password or your payment-card details through the app. Apple's handling of account, payment and purchase information is described in App Store & Privacy.

3. Rating request

After a new purchase, the app may ask iOS once, at a quiet moment after a recording has finished playing, to show Apple's standard rating prompt. The prompt belongs to iOS: iOS decides whether to show it and shows it at most three times per app in 365 days. The app does not receive a result telling it whether you submitted a rating or review through the prompt. Reviews you publish on the App Store are public and may be read by us. Unlimited listening is yours whether or not you rate. The app remembers that it has asked (Section 1) and does not ask again; restoring a purchase never triggers the prompt. You can turn such prompts off for every app in Settings → Apps → App Store → In-App Ratings & Reviews (Settings → App Store on iOS 17).

4. Analytics and diagnostics

English-Thai Learn has no app-operated usage analytics or automatic crash-report upload service.

Apple may separately provide sales or transaction reporting to developers. See App Store & Privacy. Depending on your iPhone's sharing choices, Apple may also make crash or usage diagnostics available to us. Those choices are managed in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, with Share iPhone & Watch Analytics and Share With App Developers. See App Analytics & Privacy.

We use crash reports and diagnostic information from Apple, and any copies we download, only to investigate bugs and improve English-Thai Learn. We do not use them for advertising or tracking, we do not sell them, and we do not use them for unrelated purposes. Our copies stay on the Mac we develop English-Thai Learn on and are not kept in cloud storage or backups. To investigate a bug, we may give parts of a report to an AI coding assistant; before we do, we remove anything that could identify you or your iPhone, such as a device name. We do not give these reports to anyone else. We delete our copies without undue delay once the bug they relate to is resolved or we decide it needs no fix.

Records we keep because bookkeeping and tax filing require them, such as Apple's sales or transaction reports, are kept for five years from the day after the statutory filing deadline of the relevant Korean national tax for the tax period concerned, and records of offshore transactions for seven years from the same day. Where applicable law sets a different retention period, that period applies. These periods apply only to records required for accounting and tax purposes, not to support inquiries or diagnostic reports.

문의 내용과 첨부 파일은 문의를 처리하는 데 필요한 동안만 보관합니다. 문의 처리 완료란 운영자가 답변을 마쳤고 그 문의에 더 할 일이 남지 않은 상태를 말하며, 첫 답변을 보낸 것만으로 처리가 완료되지는 않습니다. 신고한 문제의 수정 버전이 배포되면 알려 드리는 경우처럼 실제로 필요한 후속 조치가 있을 때에만 더 보관하며, 일상적인 참고를 위해 보관하지 않습니다. 문의 처리가 완료되고 필요한 후속 조치가 끝나면 해당 문의의 메일과 첨부 파일을 Gmail 휴지통을 포함해 지체 없이 삭제하고, 운영자가 관리하는 다운로드·저장·전달 사본도 함께 삭제합니다.

일반적인 기술·사용 문의는 법령상 보존 기록으로 보관하지 않습니다. 법령에 따라 기록을 보존해야 하는 경우에는 그 기록에 필요한 정보만 지원 메일함과 분리하여 법령이 정한 기간 동안 보존합니다. 예를 들어 「전자상거래 등에서의 소비자보호에 관한 법률」이 적용되는 소비자 불만·분쟁 처리 기록은 3년간 보존합니다.

운영자가 메일함에서 메일을 삭제하더라도 Google의 시스템에서 모든 사본이 즉시 지워지는 것은 아닙니다. 삭제 후 일정 기간에는 Google Workspace 관리자 도구로 복구할 수 있는 사본이 남으며, 운영자는 이러한 복구용 사본을 통상적인 지원 업무에 사용하지 않습니다. 운영자가 더 이상 복구할 수 없게 된 메일은 Google이 데이터 처리 약관에 따라 최대 180일 이내에 자체 시스템에서 삭제하며, 관련 법령이 보관을 요구하는 경우는 예외입니다.

국외 처리위탁·보관 안내. 지원 메일의 처리는 정보주체와의 계약 체결·이행에 필요한 처리위탁·보관으로, 「개인정보 보호법」 제28조의8제1항제3호가목에 따라 이 방침에 공개하여 안내합니다. Google Workspace 이메일의 저장·처리는 국외에서 이루어질 수 있습니다. 아래 국가는 Google이 공개한 목록을 기준으로 한 처리 가능 범위이며, 실제 처리는 서비스와 지원 업무에 따라 달라집니다.
